What is your typical enquiry response turnaround time?

Inquiries received during business hours (9:30 AM – 6:30 PM IST) receive a callback or email response from an assigned property advisor within 15 minutes. Inquiries submitted after-hours are queued and addressed first thing the following morning.

How do I schedule an in-person or live video property visit?

You can book tours directly from any individual property listing page, or submit the contact form above with the topic "Finding a rental property" and specify your target dates. Our concierge team coordinates entry passes and escorts you through the premises.

What should landlords prepare when requesting property management?

Please share the property address, unit layout (e.g., 2BHK, 3BHK penthouse), furnishing status, and target rental expectations. Rentora conducts a complimentary asset evaluation and prepares rental yield benchmarks before onboarding.

Do you provide remote management for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)?

Yes. Over 40% of our managed portfolios belong to NRI owners across the US, UAE, Singapore, and Europe. We manage end-to-end tenant screening, digital biometric agreement registrations, property tax payments, and NRE/NRO rent remittances.

How do active tenants submit emergency maintenance requests?

Active tenants can log tickets in real time through the Rentora Resident Dashboard, or call our 24/7 emergency dispatch helpline (+91 98400 12345) for burst pipes, electrical power outages, or lockouts. Technicians arrive within 90 minutes.

Can I visit your headquarters without a prior appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome during working hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM). However, to ensure an advisor specialized in your specific neighborhood or portfolio tier is reserved for you, we recommend scheduling 2 hours in advance.

How are security deposits and escrow funds protected?

All tenant security deposits and advance rentals are held in RBI-regulated scheduled bank escrow accounts. Funds are only settled upon joint digital sign-off and move-out condition audits, eliminating return disputes entirely.
