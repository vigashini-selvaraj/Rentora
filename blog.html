Actionable tips for smooth daily management.

Bite-sized best practices you can put into action right away to optimize your rental yields.

TIP 01

The 60-Day Renewal Window

Initiate lease renewal dialogues 60 days in advance to eliminate turnover downtime and plan rent escalations.

Reduces vacancy loss by 85%
TIP 02

Document Every Move-In Room

Capture high-resolution timestamped photos of fixtures, walls, and flooring to make security deposit settlements dispute-free.

Prevents 95% of deposit conflicts
TIP 03

Mandate Digital Auto-Debit

Transition tenants from ad-hoc manual transfers to recurring UPI/NACH auto-debit on the 1st of each calendar month.

Maintains 99%+ on-time collections
TIP 04

Pre-Monsoon Sealant Check

Audit exterior window gaskets, balcony drain pipes, and roof waterproofing every June to prevent interior moisture damage.

Saves up to ₹50,000 in repair bills
